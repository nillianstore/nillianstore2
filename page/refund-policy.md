# Refund & Return Policy

**Last updated: July 11, 2026**

At **Nillianstore**, your satisfaction is our priority. Since all purchases are fulfilled through **Amazon.ae**, returns and refunds are processed according to Amazon's policies, with our dedicated support to guide you through the process.

---

## 1. Return Period

You may return most items within **15 days** of delivery for a full refund.

| Item Type | Return Window |
|-----------|---------------|
| Standard products | 15 days from delivery |
| Damaged/defective items | 15 days from delivery (extended if reported promptly) |
| Amazon Prime items | Subject to Amazon Prime return terms |

---

## 2. Condition Requirements

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet the following conditions:

- **Unused** — the product must not have been used beyond initial inspection
- **Original packaging** — all original boxes, tags, and accessories must be included
- **Proof of purchase** — Amazon order number or receipt required
- **Same condition** — item must be in the same condition you received it

Items that are **not eligible** for return:
- Products damaged due to misuse, neglect, or improper care
- Items missing original packaging or accessories
- Gift cards
- Digital downloads
- Customized or personalized products
- Products marked as "non-returnable" on Amazon.ae

---

## 3. How to Initiate a Return

### Option A: Through Amazon.ae (Recommended)

1. Log into your **[Amazon.ae account](https://www.amazon.ae){: target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow sponsored" }**
2. Go to **"Your Orders"**
3. Find the item you wish to return
4. Click **"Return or Replace Items"**
5. Select a return reason from the dropdown
6. Choose your preferred return method:
   - Drop-off at an Amazon collection point
   - Scheduled pickup from your address
7. Print the return shipping label (if required)
8. Package the item securely and send it back

### Option B: Contact Nillianstore for Assistance

If you need help with the return process:
1. Email us at **[nillianstore@gmail.com](mailto:nillianstore@gmail.com)**
2. Include your **Amazon order number** and product details
3. Describe the issue (damaged, wrong item, not as described, etc.)
4. Attach photos if the item is damaged or defective

We will respond within **24 hours** with step-by-step guidance.

---

## 4. Refund Processing

| Step | Timeframe |
|------|-----------|
| Return initiated | You submit the return request |
| Amazon receives return | 3–7 business days (depending on return method) |
| Amazon inspects item | 1–2 business days after receipt |
| Refund approved | Within 2 business days of inspection |
| Refund credited | 5–7 business days to original payment method |

**Refund methods:**
- Refunds are issued to your **original payment method** (credit card, debit card, etc.)
- Amazon.ae gift card refunds may be processed faster (1–2 business days)

---

## 5. Exchanges

If you received a damaged, defective, or incorrect item and would like an exchange:
1. Follow the return process above (Option A or B)
2. Select **"Replace"** instead of "Refund" when prompted on Amazon.ae
3. A replacement will be shipped once the returned item is received

Exchanges are subject to product availability. If the replacement item is out of stock, a full refund will be issued instead.

---

## 6. Damaged or Defective Items

If your product arrives damaged or defective:

1. **Take photos** of the damaged product and packaging
2. **Contact us immediately** at nillianstore@gmail.com with:
   - Your Amazon order number
   - Photos of the damage
   - A brief description of the issue
3. We will coordinate with Amazon.ae to arrange a **free replacement or full refund**
4. Do not dispose of the item or packaging until the return is confirmed

**Important:** Report damaged items within **48 hours** of delivery for the fastest resolution.

---

## 7. Return Shipping Costs

| Return Reason | Shipping Cost |
|---------------|---------------|
| Item damaged or defective | **Free** — Amazon covers return shipping |
| Wrong item delivered | **Free** — Amazon covers return shipping |
| Changed mind / buyer's remorse | Return shipping costs may apply (per Amazon policy) |
| Item not as described | **Free** — Amazon covers return shipping |

Amazon.ae provides prepaid return shipping labels for eligible returns. You can drop off the package at designated collection points or schedule a pickup.

---

## 8. Partial Refunds

In some cases, Amazon may issue a **partial refund** for:
- Items returned with missing accessories or original packaging
- Items showing signs of use beyond reasonable inspection
- Products with minor cosmetic differences from the listing

You will be notified of any partial refund decision before it is processed.

---

## 9. Order Cancellation

You can cancel your order **before it has been shipped**:

1. Log into Amazon.ae
2. Go to **"Your Orders"**
3. Select the order and click **"Cancel Items"**
4. If the order has not yet been dispatched, cancellation is immediate

**After dispatch:** The order cannot be cancelled but can be returned following the standard return process above.
