**Last updated: July 11, 2026**

At **Nillianstore**, all products are fulfilled and shipped by **Amazon.ae**. This means you benefit from Amazon's reliable delivery network across the United Arab Emirates. Below is our shipping policy that explains how delivery works when you purchase products through our curated storefront.


## 1. Order Fulfillment

All orders placed through Nillianstore product links are:
- **Processed and shipped by Amazon.ae** — not by Nillianstore directly
- **Fulfilled from Amazon's UAE warehouses** — ensuring fast, reliable delivery
- **Subject to Amazon.ae's shipping policies** — which may change at Amazon's discretion

We curate and showcase products on our website, but the physical delivery is handled entirely by Amazon's logistics network.


## 2. Delivery Areas

Products are available for delivery across the **United Arab Emirates**, including:

| Emirate | Coverage |
|---------|----------|
| Dubai | All areas |
| Abu Dhabi | All areas |
| Sharjah | All areas |
| Ajman | All areas |
| Ras Al Khaimah | All areas |
| Fujairah | All areas |
| Umm Al Quwain | All areas |

**Remote area delivery** may require additional time. Amazon.ae will confirm delivery availability based on your shipping address at checkout.


## 3. Processing Time

| Step | Timeframe |
|------|-----------|
| Order confirmation | Immediate (via Amazon.ae) |
| Order processing | 1–2 business days |
| Dispatch from warehouse | Within 24 hours of processing |
| Tracking number provided | Once order is dispatched |

Processing times may vary during:
- Public holidays (Eid, National Day, etc.)
- Amazon sale events (White Friday, Prime Day)
- High-demand product launches


## 4. Delivery Timeframes

Standard delivery times within the UAE:

| Delivery Type | Timeframe | Cost |
|---------------|-----------|------|
| Standard Delivery | 2–5 business days | Per Amazon.ae rates |
| Expedited Delivery | 1–2 business days | Per Amazon.ae rates |
| Same-Day Delivery (selected areas) | Same day if ordered before cutoff | Per Amazon.ae rates |
| Friday/Saturday Delivery | Available in most areas | Per Amazon.ae rates |

**Note:** Amazon Prime members may be eligible for free expedited delivery on qualifying items.


## 5. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are determined by **Amazon.ae** at checkout based on:
- Product size and weight
- Delivery address
- Selected delivery speed
- Amazon Prime membership status (free shipping for Prime members on eligible items)

Nillianstore does not charge any additional shipping fees. All shipping costs are transparently displayed on Amazon.ae before you complete your purchase.


## 6. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched:
1. You will receive a **shipping confirmation email** from Amazon.ae
2. A **tracking link** will be provided in your Amazon account under "Your Orders"
3. Track your package in real-time on Amazon.ae or the Amazon mobile app
4. You will receive SMS/email updates at key delivery milestones

If you experience any tracking issues, contact Amazon.ae customer service directly or reach out to us at **nillianstore@gmail.com** for guidance.


## 7. International Shipping

Currently, products listed on Nillianstore are available for delivery **within the United Arab Emirates only**. International shipping is not available through our storefront.

If you are outside the UAE and interested in a product, please contact us, and we will direct you to the appropriate Amazon marketplace for your region.


## 8. Delivery Issues

If you experience any of the following delivery problems, please take the action indicated:

| Issue | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Package not delivered | Check Amazon tracking; contact Amazon support if delayed |
| Damaged package | Refuse delivery or take photos; initiate return on Amazon.ae |
| Wrong item delivered | Initiate return/exchange through Amazon.ae "Your Orders" |
| Missing items | Check if order was split into multiple shipments; contact Amazon support |
| Delivery to wrong address | Contact Amazon.ae support immediately |

For any delivery-related questions or to report issues, you can also contact Nillianstore at **nillianstore@gmail.com**, and we will help guide you through the resolution process.


## 9. Amazon Prime Benefits

If you are an **Amazon Prime** member, you may be eligible for:
- Free expedited delivery on qualifying items
- Same-day delivery in select Dubai and Abu Dhabi areas
- Priority customer service from Amazon

Prime membership is managed by Amazon.ae and is separate from Nillianstore. Check your Prime status on Amazon.ae.
