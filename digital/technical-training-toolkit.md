## What you get

You train people on things that can hurt them: hydraulics, forklifts, lockout, hot work. The course lives in your head, the quiz lives in a word doc, and the competence sign-off is "somewhere." When a client, auditor or manager asks how training went, you're reconstructing.

This toolkit holds the whole loop in one workbook: **PLAN** every module in one register with observable objectives; **TEACH** with a session plan whose timing block totals itself and flags overruns before you're in the room; **ASSESS** with a quiz builder feeding an auto-marked scoring sheet and a weighted practical observation that turns Competent / Not yet competent by formula; **IMPROVE** with trainee feedback and senior-trainer observation rubrics. The 3 PPTX decks get you teaching today — duplicate, swap in your equipment, done.

## Who it's for

Technical trainers, HSE trainers, maintenance and operations leads who deliver classroom + hands-on training and need records that prove competence without retyping a single number.

## Who it's not for

Corporate e-learning authoring or LMS admin. This is a trainer's working system: Excel plus PowerPoint, no macros.
