## What it is

LEEBKIT by LEEBLAAB turns 1–10 still images + captions into a finished vertical promo MP4 — animated stills (Ken Burns), kinetic text, transitions, voiceover and music with auto-ducking — rendered entirely on your machine. Double-click `start.bat`, open the webUI, get an MP4. **No cloud. No API. No subscription.**

**Price: FREE.** The code is AGPL-3.0 open source; presets and commercial content packs stay proprietary to LEEBLAAB.

## What v1.0 does

- 9:16 — 1080×1920 @ 30fps promo videos (15/30/60s targets)
- Drag-drop up to 10 images, reorder shots by dragging cards
- Shot planner with duration chips enforced live against the target length
- One-prompt plan generator (rule-based — no LLM, no network)
- Storyboard frames + pre-render checks, then a full render
