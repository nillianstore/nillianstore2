## What you get

The complete kit: the 7-tab workbook (also opens in Google Sheets), a 5-page A4 guide that explains the math in plain words, and three print-ready forms — a breakdown slip for shift handover, a monthly review board, and a criticality scoring card. Every figure on the dashboard is a formula over your log: durations, MTBF, MTTR, availability, Pareto bars, top-5 costs. You type facts once; nothing is calculated twice.

## Who it's for

Maintenance supervisors, reliability engineers, plant managers and shift leads in manufacturing, steel, food processing, logistics and process operations — anywhere unplanned downtime is the enemy and nobody has time for paperwork.

## Who it's not for

If you need a full CMMS with work-order scheduling, mobile apps and asset hierarchies, this isn't it. This is deliberately one workbook plus paper at the machine — the fastest route from "we keep breaking the same bearing" to a scheduled fix.
