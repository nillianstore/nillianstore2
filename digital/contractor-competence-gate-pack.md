## What you get

The complete kit: the 8-tab workbook (also opens in Google Sheets), a 5-page A4 guide that walks the six-step gate in plain words, and three print-ready forms — induction sign-off, competence assessment cover sheet, and the gate decision record that doubles as your pass certificate. ## Who it's for

HSE officers, site managers, procurement and permits-to-work issuers in industrial sites, logistics facilities, steel plants and construction — anywhere contractors enter your site and an unqualified one is an accident waiting to be investigated.

If you need a full contractor-management platform with vendor portals and automated document collection, this isn't it. This is deliberately one workbook plus paper at the gate — the fastest route from "documents by email and competence by handshake" to a system an auditor can verify in three printed pages.
