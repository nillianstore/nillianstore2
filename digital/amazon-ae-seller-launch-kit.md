## What you get

Most new Amazon.ae sellers learn the fee structure by getting surprised by it. This kit puts the current numbers — referral rates per category, FBA fulfilment tiers, storage and late-stock fees, VAT on fees — into working calculators, plus the playbook behind a real UAE storefront in teapots, kitchenware, sports, fishing and lighting.

The workbook calculates your true margin per SKU before you list; the guide covers listing tactics that actually moved the needle, realistic launch timelines, ad spend and optimum ACOS, and the doctrine that keeps small sellers alive: just start, small test quantities, push winners, drop losers.

## Who it's for

UAE-based sellers (or UAE-shipping brands) launching on Amazon.ae — FBA or FBM — who want the fee math and launch sequence done once, correctly, instead of by guesswork.

## Who it's not for

Anyone looking for "get rich" tactics or fake-review schemes. This is pricing math, listing craft and launch discipline.
