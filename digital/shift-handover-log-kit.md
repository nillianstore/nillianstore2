## What you get

At 22:00 the outgoing supervisor knows everything. At 22:05 half of it is gone — and nobody wrote down which half. This pack is the fix: a signed handover form, a production log that highlights deviations as you type them, a tracker where no open item crosses a shift boundary without an owner and a due date, and a dashboard that recalculates itself.

The 5-tab workbook ships with real-shaped sample data (rolling-mill lines, reheat furnaces, yard crane — all marked SAMPLE). Delete the samples and your own numbers appear. The 8-page guide PDF covers why handovers fail, the timed routine, wording rules that survive review, severity definitions, and the questions auditors and incident reviews ask.

## Who it's for

Shift supervisors, plant managers and operations leads in manufacturing, steel, food processing and process plants — anywhere production crosses a shift boundary and there is no digital logging system.

## Who it's not for

Teams wanting a full MES/CMMS with work-order integration. This is deliberately one workbook plus paper at the board.
